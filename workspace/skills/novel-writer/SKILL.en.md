# Web Novel Chapter Writing Guide

## Opening & Ending (the three-chapter law)

- Enter an event within the first 3 lines of every chapter: a conflict, a change, or an anomaly. Never open with scenery, memories, or exposition
- Every chapter ends on a hook: suspense, a reversal, an incoming crisis, or a new revelation that forces the reader to open the next chapter
- Chapter 1 extra: establish the protagonist's situation and core conflict within 30 seconds; the first payoff/conflict no later than 1/3 into the chapter

## Pacing & Word Budget

- Split the chapter into 3-5 beats (event units) of 20-25% each against the target word count
- Place the climax beat at 70-85% of the chapter; the last 10% belongs to the hook
- Dialogue drives plot, narration renders emotion: dialogue 30-50%; no more than 5 exchanges per dialogue block
- Short sentences for tension, long sentences for relief; in fast-paced style keep paragraphs under three lines

## Dialogue Rules

- Format: "CharacterName: (state/action) line", on its own line
- Lines must match the character's identity and current emotion; every line either advances the plot or builds character — cut greetings that do neither
- Never let dialogue restate information the reader already knows

## Emotional Gradient (against over-acting)

- Strong emotions (shrieking, sobbing, rage) appear once, at the chapter's climax only
- Everyday and transition sections react in restrained, everyday tone; emotions escalate with setup, never jump to the top
- Show shock through behavior and physiology (a half-step back, cold fingertips) rather than flat "he yelled"

## Foreshadowing

- Check the open_foreshadows list while writing: echo them explicitly when the plot naturally touches them
- New foreshadows must be concrete (an object, a line, an anomaly) — never abstract atmosphere
- A foreshadow pays off within 10 chapters of planting; finish this chapter's payoffs before the ending

## Consistency Red Lines

- Names, titles, proper nouns, and power systems must exactly match the characters list and world settings
- The protagonist's abilities never exceed the established ceiling without cause (respect the contract's usage limits)
- Timeline continuity: day/night, seasons, ages must not jump without reason
