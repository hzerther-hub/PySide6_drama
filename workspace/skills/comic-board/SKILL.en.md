# Comic Storyboard Adaptation Guide

## Panel Ground Rules

- 8-16 panels per chapter; short chapters take the low end, high-conflict chapters the high end
- Panel rhythm follows the story: opening hook 1-2 panels, conflict escalation 4-8 panels, ending cliffhanger 1-2 panels
- One distinct visual unit per panel: one action, one reaction, or one scene establishment — never two timescales in one panel
- Adjacent panels must vary shot size: three consecutive panels at the same shot size is a cardinal sin

## Per-Panel Field Rules

- description (visual): character + action + expression + background elements, one clear sentence of "who is doing what"; character styling must match the book's worldview/era
- dialogue: only the line or narration that must appear in this panel, one sentence max; taken from the script verbatim — never write new lines; silent panels are a legitimate pacing tool
- composition (camera): shot size (wide/full/medium/close/extreme close) + angle (eye-level/high/low) + compositional intent (e.g. "over-the-shoulder", "negative space for mood")
- image_prompt (English, 30-60 coherent words): subject + action + environment + lighting + composition; **no style words** (art style is injected by the system); no dialogue text in the image

## Visual Storytelling Techniques

- Extreme close-ups for emotional turning points; wide shots to establish spatial relationships; at least one establishing wide per chapter
- For dialogue-heavy scripts: split long speeches across multiple panels, inserting reaction panels — avoid a wall of talking heads
- The ending cliffhanger panel = the maximum-suspense image (frozen a heartbeat before the change lands), with one hook line
- Alternate quiet and explosive panels: no more than 3 consecutive action panels without a reaction or establishing panel to breathe

## Adaptation Trade-offs

- Psychological description → convert into expression/action/composition hints; never render text directly
- Transitional content → one establishing or transition panel carries the equivalent meaning
- Keep the strongest conflicts and reversals, sacrifice minutiae: every panel must be worth looking at
