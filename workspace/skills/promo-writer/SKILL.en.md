# Short-Drama Promo Copy Guide

## Universal Principles

- Everything starts from the conflict hook: lead with the strongest contradiction/reversal, no background setup
- Three selling-point questions: what is the biggest payoff? who identifies with it? which line grabs hardest?
- One piece of copy pushes exactly one core selling point; save the rest for other versions
- No spoiling key reversals; clickbait has a floor — the hook must be honored by the content

## Per-Platform Formats

| Platform | Form | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok/Kuaishou | voiceover/caption script, 15-45s | Hook in first 3s (suspense question / conflict image); fast conflict stacking; end with "tap the collection for all episodes" |
| Xiaohongshu (RedNote) | note style, 200-400 words | Title with emotion tag; casual recommendation + per-episode highlights; 3-5 hashtags at the end |
| WeChat Official Account | deep-dive 500-800 words | Suspenseful title; subsections on "character — reversal — payoff"; spoiler warning; end with bookmark/follow CTA |
| Bilibili | title + description | Playful but plain-spoken title; description covers highlights and update cadence |

## Output Format (multi-version)

Output at least 3 independently usable versions:
1. **Suspense**: open on the unsolved mystery, never reveal the ending
2. **Emotional**: open on the most relatable emotional conflict (e.g. "Ten years later he's back, and everyone is waiting for him to fail")
3. **Payoff list**: direct payoff checklist (face-slaps / underdog rise / sweet romance), labeled "payoff-dense"

Each version: hook (1-2 sentences) → selling points expanded (3-5 sentences) → CTA (1 sentence: tap collection / follow / bookmark).

## Language Red Lines

- Never exaggerate plot that does not exist; no vulgar, flame-baiting, or demeaning wording
- No allusions to real brands or people
- CTA must be concrete: if you say "tap the collection", do not merely say "come watch"
